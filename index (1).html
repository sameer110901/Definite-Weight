The Flaw

Aviation guesses payload mass — then pays for the guess, twice a day, on every route.

Standard Average Weights (e.g. 84 kg/passenger) make true individual mass an unknown variable. To compensate for that blindness, safety regulation mandates extreme conservatism: thousands of pounds of contingency fuel, loaded to carry a payload that may not even exist.

Direct Loss
$3.7M

Annual wasted fuel across a standard 100-aircraft fleet, burned solely to carry over-estimated contingency mass.

Regulatory Cost
$470K

CORSIA and EU ETS carbon penalties, incurred directly because of excess fuel burn tied to ghost weight.

Opportunity Cost
$100M+

Forfeited cargo revenue — high-yield pallets left on the tarmac because MTOW headroom was spent on imagined payload.

→ This isn't a micro-optimization. The Uncertainty Tax is a systemic P&L vulnerability sitting at the core of current flight planning — on every aircraft, every sector, every day.

The Solution

A data-fusion SaaS, deployed through hardware nobody notices.

Definite Weight is not a hardware vendor. Ultra-thin Weigh-In-Motion smart mats integrate directly into biometric e-gates and self-bag-drop stations, capturing exact mass during the natural 1–2 second pause passengers already make — with zero added friction and zero airport construction.

01 — CAPTURE
Invisible gate mats

Placed on solid terminal concrete beneath biometric e-gates — an unshakable baseline for ±0.5% regulatory accuracy, unlike volatile jet-bridge sensors.

02 — ENCRYPT
Strict differential privacy

No screens display the weight. No data ties to a passenger's name. Mass is captured, hashed, and added anonymously to the aircraft's total load vector.

03 — VALIDATE
SITA-grade sanity checks

Volumetric limits, edge-detection, and hardware-integrity monitoring form a "Data Gatekeeper" — any anomaly instantly reverts to the legal EASA default.

04 — DELIVER
Verified weight to EFB

A clean, verified figure reaches the pilot's Electronic Flight Bag — recalculating CG in real time if a checked bag boards without its passenger.
